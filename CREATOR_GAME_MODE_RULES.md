# Creator Game Mode Rules

## Core direction

This is a companion idle game, not the full racing game.

The game should create small daily reasons for users to interact with the 365 Motor Sales brand.

## Do

- Keep it mobile-first.
- Keep rewards micro-sized.
- Use simple 2D and idle-clicker loops.
- Make the player objective clear on every screen.
- Supplier should only drop Level 1 starter items.
- Higher item levels must come from merging.
- Unlock Performance Parts through Tuning Corner.
- Unlock Racing Gear through Test Track.
- Use route problems as the enemy: fuel, breakdown, police heat, traffic.
- Keep each game mode understandable in one sentence.

## Don’t

- Do not add real-time PVP yet.
- Do not add full 3D racing.
- Do not require steering controls.
- Do not drop high-level items directly.
- Do not copy another game's art or UI exactly.
- Do not use real car trademarks or manufacturer logos.
- Do not make rewards valuable enough to require complex anti-cheat yet.
- Do not make the game one giant scroll page.

## Approved modes for this stage

- Idle Racing
- Merge Bay
- Garage Building
- Service Orders
- Parts Delivery
- Fuel Saver
- Rough Road
- Dealer Showcase
- Ghost-style leaderboard later

## Not approved yet

- Live multiplayer racing
- Real-money rewards
- NFT/crypto items
- Real vehicle marketplace item ownership
- Real-time police chase gameplay
- Complex car physics
