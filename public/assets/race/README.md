Race visual override assets
===========================

These files are wired into the Race/Pixi and Road Runner canvases. Replace them with final PNG/WebP/SVG art using the same filenames to reskin the mode without touching code.

Pixi race scene sprite assets:

- `backgrounds/street_loop.png`
- `backgrounds/parts_delivery.png`
- `backgrounds/rough_road.png`
- `backgrounds/fuel_saver.png`
- `backgrounds/dealer_showcase.png`
- `cars/starter_compact.png`
- `fx/road_strip.png`
- `fx/speed_streaks.png`
- `fx/tap_boost_ring.png`
- `fx/checkpoint_flag.png`
- `fx/warning_panel.png`
- `fx/warning_badge.png`

Recommended Pixi sizes: route backgrounds `900x230`, road strip `512x116`, starter car around `260x160`, and FX on transparent PNGs. Keep the lower third readable for the car, road, rewards, and warnings.

- `routes/rough.svg`
- `routes/showcase.svg`
- `routes/track.svg`
- `routes/barangay.svg`
- `routes/farm.svg`
- `routes/mountain.svg`

Recommended size: 900x520 or wider. Keep the lower third mostly open so the road, car, rewards, and warnings remain readable.
